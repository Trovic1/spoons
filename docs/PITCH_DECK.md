# Pitch deck outline (6 slides, about 2 minutes)

Use this only if a live pitch or slide upload is requested. Taskforge asks for a Devpost page and a demo, so the video carries the pitch. One idea per slide; the screenshots are in `docs/`.

1. **Spoons: plan your day by energy, not hours.**
   Visual: the battery from the app, full width.
   Say: "For students whose energy changes every day."

2. **The problem**
   - To-do apps assume the same capacity every day.
   - With a chronic illness, a 10-task plan on a 4-spoon morning means a crash, and tomorrow is lost too.
   - Many people already describe this as "spoons" (Christine Miserandino, 2003).

3. **How it works**
   Three steps with three screenshots:
   1. Check in with today's spoons.
   2. The battery fills with what fits, with a reserve kept back.
   3. Everything else gets a next step: tomorrow, part one, a help message, or more time.

4. **It adapts**
   - Low Power mode on 3-spoon days: must-dos only.
   - Log real costs and the day re-plans.
   - Learns what repeated tasks cost you.

5. **Built accessible, built solid**
   - 0 axe WCAG 2.2 AA violations; keyboard, screen reader announcements, read-aloud, high contrast, Lexend, dark mode.
   - Planning engine written test-first: 26 tests.
   - No account, works offline, data never leaves the device.

6. **What's next**
   - Test with chronically ill students and disability-support offices.
   - Help circle for sending requests straight to friends.
   - Weekly patterns; installable app.
   - Close on the live demo link and the repo.
